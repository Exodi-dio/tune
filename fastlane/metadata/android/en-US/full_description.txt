Tune is an Apple Music-inspired local music player for Android.
It plays the music stored on your device. There is no account, no
streaming service, no sync, and no advertising or analytics.

Browse and search tracks, artists, albums, genres, composers, and
playlists. Build queues with shuffle and repeat, find synced lyrics,
tune the sound with the equalizer, and give playlists and artists your
own cover art. The interface follows the system theme and speaks twelve
languages.
